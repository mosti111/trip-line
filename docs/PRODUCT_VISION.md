# Trip Line product vision

Current experience direction, consolidated 3 October 2026. Read [AI_HANDOFF.md](AI_HANDOFF.md)
for status and [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) for the broader unfinished platform contract.

## Product

Trip Line is a premium travel discovery and inquiry experience: cinematic, editorial,
spatial, confident, crafted and interactive. Be expressive without sacrificing usability
and premium without cliché luxury. The Bronze Journey continues the approved route/ribbon identity.

## Market emphasis

Türkiye is the primary discovery story. Preserve Istanbul, Trabzon, Rize & Ayder,
Uzungöl, Antalya, Bodrum, Fethiye, Marmaris, Sapanca, Bursa, Yalova and Cappadocia.
The ten-country international directory remains secondary: Türkiye, Georgia, Egypt,
Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Maldives, Malaysia and Indonesia.
Nine source-based services support personal travel, Hajj/Umrah and business/group needs.
English, Arabic and Turkish are public locale choices; Arabic uses actual RTL with natural geography.

## Visitor journey

Discover → become inspired → understand services → explore destinations → build context
→ inquire through a clear contact/WhatsApp path. The current journey builder prepares
context for contact. Inquiry validates and prepares an editable WhatsApp/email message;
the visitor reviews and sends it in their chosen app. No server receives it through this frontend.
Reservations, payment, traveler accounts and booking infrastructure must not be implied
until actually implemented and verified.

## Experience quality

Avoid a generic tourism template, SaaS-looking grid, static brochure, identical cards
throughout, gratuitous glassmorphism, excessive gradients, meaningless motion and stock
luxury travel clichés. Identity comes from typography, editorial composition, pacing,
cinematic media, depth, controlled 3D, responsive movement, geographic discovery,
the route/ribbon language and the actual stacked Trip Line brand.

Keep source-based content honest. Profile figures retain 2025 attribution; partner
relationships, media and legal wording require publication confirmation. Never invent
prices, inventory, reviews or company claims. Noindex remains enabled.

## Mobile principle

Mobile is deliberately composed: intentional cropping, typography reflow, adjusted pacing,
touch-first interaction and simplified depth where necessary, with preserved narrative quality.
Read [responsive rules](RESPONSIVE_RULES.md) and [motion rules](MOTION_SYSTEM.md).

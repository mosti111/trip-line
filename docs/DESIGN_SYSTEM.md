## Composition and interaction

Use deliberate type scale, asymmetric editorial rhythm, photography, negative space and
thin route/Flight Trace detail. Existing scenes do not authorize generic card grids,
gradients, unrelated 3D or another visual system. Shared Photo/sections and page-family
primitives preserve coherence without identical layouts everywhere.

The homepage uses a photographic Türkiye Atlas with geographic overlay and one source-based
partner billboard. Profile presence is not a partnership claim. See [assets](ASSETS.md),
[numbers/partners](NUMBERS_AND_PARTNERS_SECTIONS.md) and frontend-v2/public/media/sources.json.
Retain 2025 attribution/legal notes; never invent prices, reviews, partners or statistics.

Phones have independent framing, caption space, reachable controls and optional stable map UI.
Tablet and short landscape require direct review. [Responsive](RESPONSIVE_RULES.md) and
[motion](MOTION_SYSTEM.md) rules govern fallbacks/interactions. Preserve focus, semantics,
keyboard access and button alternatives. Essential content needs no hover, precise drag,
WebGL or motion.

[Approved direction](APPROVED_DESIGN_DIRECTION.md) records initial dated approvals;
[decisions](DECISIONS.md) records later superseding instructions. Sculpture, serif and early
tokens remain in the [historical design document](archive/historical-material/pre-v1-consolidation/docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).
They are not active redesign instructions. Current client creative review remains open.
